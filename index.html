About

The Data Steward & Storyteller

Most people see data as a collection of cold, sterile numbers, but I see it as a historical narrative of human lived experience. I’ve spent over 12 years in the healthcare and insurance sectors—from actuarial modeling to building machine learning systems.

I combine the precision of data science with the nuance of visual arts and storytelling to "humanize" data. Whether I’m designing and operationalizing the data evaluation framework for a housing initiative that saves $1.5M or building a complex case management risk engine that boosts high cost member identification by 25%, I prioritize the historical context and the ethical implications.

I’m an artist, a woodworker, a sewer, and a student of culture; I bring that same craftsmanship and curiosity to data, ensuring that as we innovate, we never lose the human touch that prevents AI from becoming a "black box."
